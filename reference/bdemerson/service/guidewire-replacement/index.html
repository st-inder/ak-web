Guidewire Replacement

Replace Guidewire InsuranceSuite with an agentic core system your carrier owns. We start with your five-year numbers and a working prototype of one of your own products, and we tell you plainly when staying on Guidewire is the cheaper call.
Book a working session
Definition

What is Guidewire replacement?

Guidewire replacement retires PolicyCenter, BillingCenter, and ClaimCenter one line of business at a time and moves policies, billing, and claims onto a core system the carrier owns. Guidewire's fiscal 2026 annual report says it prices core subscriptions on direct written premium, that initial terms generally run five years, and that implementation and testing typically take six to 24 months or longer. The same report lists customers' internally developed solutions, "which may be accelerated by evolving AI technologies," first among Guidewire's competitors. An agentic core is that kind of system: policy, billing, and claims services on your own product model, with AI agents doing configuration, testing, data mapping, and routine servicing under human approval, running in your cloud account at a cost that grows with usage instead of premium.

What does Guidewire replacement include?

Five-year cost model

We rebuild the five-year cost of staying on Guidewire from your invoices, contracts, and staffing, then compare it with migrating to Guidewire Cloud and with replacing. You see the subscription ramp, the integrator and staff costs, the next upgrade, and the month a replacement breaks even before anyone writes code.

Working prototype on your product

Before you commit to a program, we build one of your products on the new core: one line in one state, with your rates, rules, and forms, running quote, bind, issue, an endorsement, and first notice of loss. Your underwriters and claims staff test it on their own scenarios.

Agentic core build

Policy, billing, and claims services run on one data model that matches your products, with products defined as versioned data and every change recorded as an event. AI agents draft product changes, generate tests, and handle routine servicing, and a person approves anything that binds, pays, or changes coverage.

Data conversion and parallel run

Policies in force, billing histories, and open claims move with agent-assisted mapping and automated reconciliation. Each line runs in parallel with Guidewire until the numbers match, and existing policies convert at renewal so each one moves once.

Line-by-line migration

Lines move one at a time, and within a line, state by state, so every cutover is small enough to reverse. We plan the schedule backward from your Guidewire term date, so the last line leaves before a renewal you do not need.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can you replace Guidewire without a big-bang cutover?

Yes. Lines of business move one at a time, and within a line, state by state. New business moves on the go-live date, existing policies convert at renewal, and open claims stay in ClaimCenter until they close or reconcile, so each cutover is small enough to reverse.

How long does a Guidewire replacement take?

It depends on the number of lines and states. In our planning scenario for a carrier with three lines of business, the first line goes live in month 12, the third in month 22, and Guidewire is out of production in month 25. We plan the schedule backward from your Guidewire term date.

What does replacing Guidewire cost compared with staying?

In our planning scenario, staying costs $40.2 million over five years and replacing costs $33.4 million, with breakeven in month 44 and a year-five run rate of $2.3 million against $7.3 million. Your result depends on your contract dates, lines, and run costs, which is why the first deliverable is your own five-year model.

What happens to our Guidewire contract?

Guidewire's initial subscription terms generally run five years, with annual renewals after that. You keep paying until the term ends, so we time the last cutover before a renewal date and model any months of overlap explicitly.

Do you implement Guidewire or Guidewire Cloud?

No. We do not implement or resell Guidewire or any other suite. If the five-year model shows that staying on Guidewire or migrating to Guidewire Cloud is the better call, we tell you and recommend revisiting the case before your next renewal.

What is an agentic core system?

An agentic core is a policy, billing, and claims system built on the carrier's own product model, where AI agents do configuration, testing, data mapping, and routine servicing under defined authority. A person approves any action that binds coverage, pays money, or changes terms, and every agent action is logged.

How do you govern AI agents in a regulated core?

We build the controls the NAIC Model Bulletin on AI expects into the core: an inventory of every agent and model, an authority check before each action, human approval for binding, payment, and coverage decisions, testing for unfair discrimination, and an audit trail that ties each action to the rule that allowed it.
