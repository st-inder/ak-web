Sell-Side M&A Advisory, Exit Readiness & Vendor Due Diligence

Fix the issues a buyer would find, before they find them, and go to market with a clean, credible story.
Contact us
Definition

What is sell-side M&A advisory?

Sell-side M&A advisory helps owners, management teams, private equity sponsors, and boards prepare a business for a successful sale. BD Emerson combines exit readiness, quality of earnings, working capital and debt-like analysis, tax planning, valuation, vendor due diligence, and transaction execution so sellers can address issues before buyers raise them. The result is a more organized process, a clearer value narrative, and better visibility into issues that may affect price or certainty.

Services

What does exit readiness include?

Preparation covers the areas buyers scrutinize most, so issues are addressed before they affect price or certainty.

Quality of earnings and financial readiness
Tax readiness
Commercial and operational readiness
Data-room and management readiness

Quality of earnings and financial readiness

We assess recurring revenue, customer concentration, margins, non-recurring items, EBITDA adjustments, working capital, net debt, debt-like items, and forecast assumptions. The objective is to create a clear, supportable financial story before buyers begin diligence.

  • Normalized EBITDA and adjustment support
  • Revenue quality and customer concentration analysis
  • Working capital and net debt baselines
  • Forecast support and bridge to historical results
  • Data book preparation for buyer diligence
  • Accounting policy and close process review

Tax readiness

Tax work can identify hidden exposures, refund opportunities, tax attributes, structure alternatives, tax deferral, and rate-planning opportunities. Early analysis can help the seller understand after-tax proceeds and address items that might otherwise become a buyer negotiation point.

  • Filing history and exposure review across jurisdictions
  • Sales and use tax nexus assessment
  • Worker classification and payroll tax review
  • Tax attributes: losses, credits, and basis
  • Structure alternatives and after-tax proceeds modeling
  • Pre-sale remediation and voluntary disclosure planning
  • Purchase agreement tax provision support

Commercial and operational readiness

We help management articulate market position, customer quality, growth opportunities, operating performance, and value drivers. Operational questions can be prioritized so the seller knows which processes, systems, contracts, or capabilities need attention before the sale.

  • Market position and value driver articulation
  • Customer retention and concentration analysis
  • Contract base and change-of-control review
  • Operational process and systems assessment
  • Supply chain and vendor dependency review
  • Key person dependency and retention planning
  • Growth story supported with evidence

Data-room and management readiness

A buyer-ready process requires consistent information, clear ownership of requests, and management prepared to answer questions. We can help organize diligence materials, reconcile definitions, identify gaps, and prepare executives for the themes likely to shape the buyer conversation.

  • Data room structure and document indexing
  • Consistent definitions across all financial materials
  • Diligence request ownership and tracking
  • Management presentation preparation
  • Q&A rehearsal on likely buyer themes
  • Gap identification and remediation before launch
Our approach

How does BD Emerson support the sale process?

01

Assess

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is sell-side M&A advisory?

Sell-side M&A advisory helps a company or owner prepare for and execute a sale. It can include exit readiness, quality of earnings, tax planning, valuation, vendor due diligence, data-room preparation, management support, agreement considerations, and post-close transition planning.

What is vendor due diligence?

Vendor due diligence is an independent review commissioned by the seller before marketing a business. It typically covers financial performance, tax, commercial, or operational matters and gives buyers a credible baseline while helping the seller identify and address issues early.

What is included in a sell-side quality of earnings report?

A sell-side QoE report commonly analyzes recurring revenue, customer concentration, margins, non-recurring items, EBITDA adjustments, working capital, net debt, debt-like items, accounting policies, and forecast assumptions. The scope should reflect the likely buyer questions and transaction structure.

How far in advance should a company prepare for a sale?

Preparation should begin as soon as a sale becomes a realistic possibility. The appropriate lead time depends on reporting quality, complexity, carve-out needs, tax issues, and the desired process. Early preparation creates more options to remediate issues rather than explain them under deadline.

Can BD Emerson help with carve-out financials?

Yes. Carve-out work can address shared costs, intercompany transactions, corporate overhead allocation, standalone reporting, systems, contracts, people, and other requirements for a business being separated from a larger organization. The work can also support TSA design and Day 1 standalone readiness.

Does sell-side preparation guarantee a higher price?

No advisor can guarantee a price. Preparation can improve clarity, reduce avoidable uncertainty, support a credible value narrative, identify value drivers, and help the seller address issues that might otherwise reduce confidence or create negotiation friction. It is a process improvement, not a promise of a particular valuation.

Does BD Emerson support owners after closing?

Yes. Depending on the engagement, support can include closing mechanics, tax and estate planning, integration or separation, reporting transition, and post-transaction value realization for owners, executives, and the business. The scope can continue into integration, separation, or other transition priorities.
