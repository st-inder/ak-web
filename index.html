FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does AK Web Agency offer managed cybersecurity services?

Yes, AK Web Agency is a trusted managed cyber security services provider that offers full-cycle solutions across cybersecurity management, governance, risk and compliance, and technology.

Do you work with small and medium-sized businesses?

Yes, AK Web Agency has a range of specialized cybersecurity and advisory services for small and medium-sized businesses, recognizing their specific needs, compliance requirements, budget limitations.

Does AK Web Agency advise on mergers and acquisitions?

Yes. AK Web Agency runs buy-side and sell-side due diligence, quality of earnings, carve-out and divestiture support, TSA scoping, and post-merger integration. Cyber and technology diligence is performed by the same practitioners who do that work for operating clients.

Do you help with AI governance and AI-related cyber risk management?

Yes, AI governance is one of AK Web Agency's primary competencies. We advise on cybersecurity frameworks and regulations such as ISO 42001, EU AI Act, NIS 2, and DORA, while also offering AI governance as a service.

Does AK Web Agency offer virtual CISO services?

Yes, AK Web Agency's virtual CISOs are trusted advisors who step in to flexibly help internal teams to fortify security posture, guard against cyberattacks, and lead compliance programs without the need for a full-time CISO.

When should we bring in an outside consulting firm?

Bring in outside help when a decision or deadline outpaces internal capacity: a new compliance requirement, a security incident, a major technology migration, or a transaction. AK Web Agency scopes engagements to the decision at hand, so you get senior expertise without standing overhead.
