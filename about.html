ABOUT

One firm for engineering, AI, cybersecurity, compliance, and systems.

AK Web Agency puts senior practitioners on the work. The person who scopes the engagement stays close to the build, the control design, and the handover.

Our mission

Help operators ship systems they can defend, explain, and afford. We would rather leave a smaller, documented result than a program that depends on us staying in the room.

How an engagement starts

A working session, a written scope, and a named owner on our side. If the problem is a different shape than the one in the first email, we say so before a proposal goes out.

Advantages

Senior people on the file

Architecture, security, and assurance decisions are made by the practitioners doing the work, not handed down from a separate slide team.

Evidence prepared with the build

Control notes, diagrams, and test records are part of delivery. An audit later should not require a second project to reconstruct what happened.

Security that changes the design

Findings go back into architecture, access, and logging. A report that nobody can implement is not the deliverable.

AI with an owner and a limit

We define the use case, the data it may touch, how it is evaluated, and the spend cap before anything reaches production.

Delivery principles

Write it down

Decisions, assumptions, and owners live in the working record, not in a meeting that nobody minuted.

Smallest useful scope

We cut work that does not change the risk, the system, or the bill.

Show the evidence

Claims about security or savings come with the artifact that supports them.

Leave it runnable

Access, docs, and the next 90 days are part of done.
